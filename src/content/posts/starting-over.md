This site is my place to write without the noise of social media — a simple journal for thoughts, photos, book notes, and the ordinary moments worth keeping.

The earlier version lived on Hostinger. A bad publish wiped the posts and scrambled the homepage. Rather than keep fighting that platform, I’m rebuilding here, with every entry saved as a file in GitHub so nothing can vanish on a careless click.

If you were looking for the three or four earlier posts: they’re gone from the old host, and I’m rewriting from memory as I go. This page is the new beginning.

Welcome — or welcome back.
